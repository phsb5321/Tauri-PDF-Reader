Lectrice

Every page, read aloud.

lec·trice · the reader that reads to you

Lectrice is French for a person employed to read aloud to someone. The app is your lectrice: a local-first desktop PDF reader that reads documents aloud — highlight a passage, press play, and let it turn the page for you.

named: French “reads aloud to you” vetted out: VoxPage (own ext) · Lectern/Lector (taken) palette: Catppuccin blue = app · mauve = voice

On Reading Aloud

For most of history, reading was reading aloud. Silent reading is the late invention; the page was always meant to be heard.

Lectrice holds onto that — it highlights the sentence it is speaking and keeps it in view, so your eyes can rest while the words keep moving.

When the page ends, it turns the page for you, and reads on.

07 Voice & principles

01

Local-first, quiet

No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry chrome. The document is the hero.

02

The voice is the feature

Blue is the app, mauve is the voice. The speaking state is never hidden.

03

Developer-grade restraint

Mono for data, one accent, sharp geometry. Nothing decorative without information.

04

Honest surfaces

A real page, a real waveform, real progress. No fake book spines.
