## Notes

- All 5 PDFs unencrypted, no linearization, no syntax errors per qpdf. Zburivsky is the
  repair-warning edge case (Name/Number tree) — a deliberate corpus member: it exercises
  pdf.js tolerance of slightly-malformed trees.
- Range: 6.8–24.5 MB, 337–613 pages — includes the two largest real books from the
  track-D Mac library (Kleppmann 613p, Konieczny 375p, Reis 544p, Kimball 601p).
- The EPUB (row 6) is the negative control for the open flow's PDF filter; the open
  journey must refuse it (filter is `.pdf` only — verify in-app).
- First-page renders are 40 dpi PNGs, deterministic per qpdf/poppler version; they are
  _poppler_ renders, NOT the pdf.js cover golden — the packaged journey asserts real
  pdf.js page-1 pixels (cover golden discipline, environment-bound per plan-review).
- Copyrighted books remain local: source dir is outside the repo; nothing in this
  manifest is a book excerpt.

## Regenerate

From the corpus root (see `LECTRICE_REAL_PDF_CORPUS`):

```bash
for f in *.pdf; do sha256sum "$f"; pdfinfo "$f" | grep -E 'Pages|Encrypted|Page size|PDF version'; qpdf --check "$f"; done
pdftoppm -f 1 -l 1 -png -r 40 "$f" /tmp/corpus-render/$(basename "$f" | tr ' ()' '___')
```

Machine-readable metadata (basename → sha256 + pages) lives in
`manifest-2026-08-13.json` — the runner joins it for the page-count assertion.
